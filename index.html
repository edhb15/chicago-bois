HOW TO PLAY

  • Pick CHARACTER in the main menu to choose a ready-made character or make your own: body, height, skin, hair, beard, hat, shades, clothes and colours.
  • Every crime fills the wanted meter. Small stuff gives +10%, robberies +25–40%, the bank vault and the armored truck +60%.
  • Fill all five stars to win. Each star makes the cops faster, smarter and meaner – at five stars a chopper joins the hunt.
  • Cops who see you will chase. Break line of sight, duck into alleys, hide in dumpsters or bushes, or buy a disguise to escape.
  • Shops and apartments with an open door can be walked into. Robberies happen at the counter inside; in apartments, hold E at the dresser to search for valuables. Careful – cops follow you in.
  • You always carry a weapon. With one out, walk up to anyone – even a cop – and hold E to rob them. The bat and crowbar smash props and car windows; the pistol (12 shots, then it reloads) knocks people flat – they get back up – and shoots up cars. Gunfire brings the police fast.
  • If officers grab you, the arrest meter fills. Shove them away or run. If it fills up – or your health hits zero – you're BUSTED.
  • Crimes done while cops are chasing you give a +25% heat bonus. Risky… but fast.
